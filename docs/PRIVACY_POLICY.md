# Privacy Policy for Klemat

**Effective date:** June 11, 2026

Klemat ("the app", "we", "us") is an Arabic word game. This policy explains what
information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using
Klemat you agree to this policy.

If you have questions, contact us at: **abedomar2004@gmail.com**

---

## 1. Information we collect

**Account information.** When you create an account or sign in, we collect and
store:

- If you sign up with **email and password**: your email address. Passwords are
  handled and stored by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store
  your raw password.
- If you sign in with **Google**: your name, email address, and profile
  information that Google provides to us through Google Sign-In.
- If you continue as a **guest**: no email or name is collected. Firebase
  assigns an anonymous identifier so your progress can be saved on that device.

**Game data.** We store your gameplay data in Google Firestore, including: your
in-game username, level progress, win/loss statistics and streaks, points,
in-game diamonds, and the list of words you have completed.

**Leaderboard.** Your **username** and **points** are visible to other players on
the in-app leaderboard. Do not use a username that reveals personal information
you do not wish to share publicly.

**Usage and device data.** We use Google Firebase Analytics, which automatically
collects standard analytics data such as app interactions, device model,
operating system version, and approximate region. This helps us understand how
the app is used and improve it.

**Settings stored on your device.** Your language, theme, and sound/haptic
preferences are stored only on your device (not on our servers).

---

## 2. How we use your information

We use the information above to:

- Create and manage your account and sign you in.
- Save and sync your game progress, statistics, and leaderboard standing.
- Operate, maintain, and improve the app.
- Diagnose problems and analyze usage trends.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do **not** use it for
third-party advertising.

---

## 3. Third-party services

Klemat relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own
privacy policy:

- **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics) —
  https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- **Google Sign-In** (only if you choose it) —
  https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Almaany** — when you view a word's definition, the app requests that
  definition from almaany.com. Only the word itself is sent; no personal
  information is shared. https://www.almaany.com
- **Forvo** — if you tap to hear a word's pronunciation, a link to forvo.com is
  opened. https://forvo.com

---

## 4. Data retention and deletion

We retain your account and game data for as long as your account exists.

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by
emailing **abedomar2004@gmail.com** from the email address associated with
your account. We will delete your data within a reasonable period after
verifying the request.

---

## 5. Children's privacy

Klemat is a general-audience word game and is not directed at children under the
age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly
collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided
us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

---

## 6. Security

We use Google Firebase's infrastructure to store your data. Access to user data
is restricted by security rules so that a signed-in user can only modify their
own account. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take
reasonable measures to protect your information.
